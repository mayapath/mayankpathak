EDISENSE — Enterprise AI
Knowledge Platform

EDISENSE is an always-on enterprise AI assistant designed and built from the ground up — letting employees ask questions in plain English across complex healthcare SaaS documentation, eliminating manual searches, SME drain, and escalation bottlenecks. Built on Microsoft Copilot Studio with RAG pipelines and Claude Code, EDISENSE intelligently routes queries across Smart Trading, Enrollment, FHIR, and SaaS platform domains.
