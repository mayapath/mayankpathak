Flagship system

EDISENSE is the proof point.

A role-aware enterprise AI experience designed around one idea: make complex healthcare SaaS knowledge easier to find, understand, and act on — without collapsing governance, identity, or technical depth into a single chatbot.

Enterprise agent system · Healthcare SaaS

From scattered knowledge to guided execution.

EDISENSE combines role-aware orchestration, specialist agents, enterprise knowledge, MCP-based tools, retrieval, identity, and cloud services into one coherent experience. The objective is practical productivity: reduce time spent hunting for answers, surface the right knowledge in context, and help users move through technical work with less friction.

My AI operating model

The model is only one layer.

The hardest part of enterprise AI is not getting an LLM to answer. It is engineering the system around it so the experience stays useful, secure, observable, governable, and economically sensible as usage grows.

01

Business & workflow

Identify the bottleneck, the user, the decision, the friction, and the productivity outcome before choosing an AI pattern.

02

Knowledge & tools

Ground in trusted sources and expose narrow capabilities through MCP, APIs, functions, and structured domain data.

03

Trust & control

Design identity, RBAC, OAuth, safety boundaries, controlled writes, human review, and auditability into the architecture.

04

Adoption & economics

Measure usefulness, failure modes, latency, token consumption, model routing, and whether the system is actually improving work.
