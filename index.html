Flagship Project

EDISENSE — Enterprise AI
Knowledge Platform

EDISENSE is an always-on enterprise AI assistant I designed and built from the ground up — letting employees ask questions in plain English across complex healthcare SaaS documentation, eliminating manual searches, SME drain, and escalation bottlenecks.

Built on Microsoft Copilot Studio with RAG pipelines and Claude Code, EDISENSE intelligently routes queries across Smart Trading, Enrollment, FHIR, and SaaS platform domains — delivering grounded, role-specific answers in seconds.

In its first 70 days, EDISENSE handled 5,200 conversation sessions, saved 767 hours of productivity, and auto-resolved 63% of queries without any human intervention.
